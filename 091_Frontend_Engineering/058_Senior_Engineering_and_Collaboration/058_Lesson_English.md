# 058 — Senior engineering and collaboration

[Persian](./058_Lesson_Persian.md) · [Course path](../README_English.md) · [Execution guide](../RUNNING_English.md)

## Concept and mechanism

Seniority is demonstrated by reliable judgment and team outcomes, not the number of libraries known. Clarify ambiguous requirements, divide work into reviewable changes, identify risks early, and communicate tradeoffs in terms of user impact and maintenance.

Review for correctness, accessibility, security, performance, and understandable ownership. Distinguish blocking defects from preferences and explain the reason for a suggestion. Mentor by helping colleagues reason, not by rewriting everything. During incidents, mitigate user impact, communicate facts, preserve evidence, and follow with a blameless analysis and actionable prevention. Estimate uncertainty explicitly and negotiate scope instead of hiding risk.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse a large refactor with delivered value. A review comment without rationale is hard to learn from or prioritize.

## Exercise and acceptance criteria

Review the task board as if a colleague proposed it. Write three prioritized findings with trigger, impact, and concrete fix, then propose a small improvement.

## Solution guidance

Focus first on data loss, unsafe input handling, and broken keyboard behavior. Explain evidence and suggest verification that protects the user workflow.
